About the Author

André De Warr

They say history is written by the victors, the famous, and the extraordinary. But what about the rest of us?

André De Warr writes about ordinary lives, difficult choices, family, regret, hope and the roads that shape us. His work is drawn to the people who may never make the headlines, but whose lives are no less complicated, painful, funny or meaningful.

His books range from deeply personal reflection to emotional fiction and travel writing. The Troubles and Tribulations in the Life of a Nobody looks back on some of the hardest periods of his life, The Long Road Home explores family, consequences and the possibility of finding a way back, while Northbound follows a motorcycle journey through Europe and the freedom, humour and discovery found on the open road.

Whether writing from experience, imagination or the journey itself, André’s aim is simple: to tell honest, human stories about people who might otherwise go unnoticed.

Delivery, returns & privacy

Information for customers buying signed books directly from André De Warr Books.

Delivery

Signed-book orders are currently available for delivery to UK addresses only.

  • UK delivery is included in the price shown.
  • Orders placed with a non-UK delivery address will be cancelled and refunded.
  • International customers can order standard copies through the Amazon links on this website.
  • Orders are usually dispatched within 3–5 working days.
  • Delivery details are collected securely during PayPal checkout.

Returns & cancellations

You may normally cancel an online order within 14 days of receiving it, without giving a reason.

  • Please email us within 14 days of delivery if you wish to cancel.
  • After telling us you wish to cancel, please return the book within 14 days.
  • Unless the item is faulty, damaged or incorrect, you are responsible for return postage.
  • Refunds are processed in line with your statutory consumer rights.

Privacy

We use the personal information supplied with your order only where needed to process and fulfil your purchase, answer enquiries and meet legal or accounting obligations.

  • This may include your name, email address, delivery address and order details.
  • Payment processing is handled by PayPal; we do not receive your full card or bank details.
  • Order information may be shared with postal or delivery providers only where needed to send your purchase.
  • Information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for fulfilment and legal or accounting purposes.
